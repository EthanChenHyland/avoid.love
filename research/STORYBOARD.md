# Film treatment — Things you kept

Ten chapters, five principal sequences. Total intended desktop travel ~28–34 screens; mobile ~18–22, tuned after footage. A continuous fixed visual stage is driven by a semantic narrative track. Source artwork precedes scene implementation. Each beat includes a hold; no uniform section rhythm.

## First milestone
Build only 00 → petal transition → 01 initially. Evaluate desktop screenshots, reverse scrolling, adapted mobile, keyboard navigation, reduced motion and asset readiness before adding other chapters. Hero A preferred provisionally; choose after viewing generations.

## Five sequence requirements
Waiting must include time/focus progression; Unsent must contain formation, erasure and physical paper change; Us needs at least three matched memories; Distance must make separation spatial; Impossible must recall recognizably established objects. Static scenes alone do not meet these requirements.

## Sound
Silent by default. No sound control unless an actual authored audio track exists. Intimacy must work without audio.
