# AVOID.LOVE — Things you kept

A film told from the other chair. A visitor supplies the person; objects supply evidence that they mattered. Photographic intimacy, with impossible changes of scale when memory takes over. No identifiable protagonists. No fake institution. No borrowed Pear imagery or fonts.

## Photographic rules
- Desktop plates: 16:9, 2K masters; mobile: authored 9:16 or deliberate 4:5 crops with object above copy. Do not center-crop blindly.
- 50mm at seated eye/table height for rooms; 100mm macro for flowers and paper. No ultra-wide fake interiors. Only the climax violates physical scale.
- 35mm negative, fine grain and gentle halation, contemporary objects with lived-in texture. No sepia vintage preset.
- Tungsten 2800K practical light against blue-hour 6500K window light. Early scenes warm, waiting/distance blue, ending pale morning sun.
- Scarlet poppy #ba281f, oxblood #541923, ink #14191c, soft ivory #f3ece2, muted amber #b77d45. Red always belongs to an object.
- Shadows retain material detail. Blacks may be deep at edges, never flatten the dominant object. Narrow focus planes, credible lenses, restrained bloom.
- Flower: one imperfect red poppy, translucent wrinkled petals, dark stamen, bent green stem. A romantic token; never a bouquet advertisement.
- Paper: thin cream paper, physical folds, deckled wear, real cast shadows. Generated artwork contains no typeset text; semantic text added in browser.
- People: absent. Seats, two cups, reflections, belongings indicate presence. No named couple, demographics or stock kisses.
- Persistent motif: the poppy becomes petal, pressed flower, red reflection, then living flower again. Use a folded note as the second recurring object.

## Typography
Bodoni Moda (OFL) for enormous emotional words, italic only for intimacy. Manrope (OFL) for short readable captions and controls. No clinical mono grid. Intentional foreground occlusion of the word “love” against a physical petal; always keep the semantic title accessible.

## Hero alternatives (evaluate as artwork before code)
A / A flower you kept: immense scarlet poppy against an actual blue dusk field, petal cutout crosses oversized ivory love. Push into its folds; emerge on café table beside a smaller poppy. Strongest scale and transition potential.
B / The other chair: rain-soaked café, two cups cooling. Quiet and recognizable; less immediate visual surprise. Best as second scene.
C / Everything you didn't say: warm room with suspended folded letters, one scarlet ribbon. Architectural paper scale. More surreal; reserve for Unsent/Impossible if it risks becoming a fantasy wallpaper.

## Motion production
Do not call a still-image camera move generated video. Bake-off is still generation. A short original rendered sequence from accepted artwork can validate deterministic scroll playback. Genuine Seedance film requires a verified provider credential; none found in the supplied local credential file. Prepare start/end frames and direction, retain strong poster fallback.

## Acceptance
Reject malformed cups, impossible vase stems, slick stock styling, plastic petals, random gibberish, excessive orange grading, and scenic images with no romantic evidence. Evaluate full viewport and 390px composition. Scores are critique, not proof of showcase parity.
